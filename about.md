# About Time–Space Oscillations (TSO)

Time–Space Oscillations (TSO) is a theoretical framework that explores whether relativistic, quantum, and gravitational phenomena can emerge from intrinsic oscillatory structures associated with particles and spacetime.

# Why TSO?

Modern physics successfully describes nature through several powerful frameworks, including quantum mechanics, relativity, and electromagnetism. However, these frameworks employ fundamentally different conceptual descriptions and remain difficult to reconcile within a single unified picture.

TSO explores whether these phenomena emerge from a common oscillatory structure associated with particles and spacetime itself.

# What makes TSO different?

- Particles are associated with intrinsic Time–Space fields.
- Quantum phenomena emerge from oscillatory relationships rather than fundamental wave-particle duality.
- Relativistic and gravitational effects arise from changes in oscillatory structure.
- Measurement is interpreted as a synchronization process. In TSO, quantum-mechanical behavior emerges from the dynamics of oscillators evolving through closed temporal structures.

# Who should read TSO?
- Physicists interested in alternative foundations.
- Engineers seeking an intuitive geometric interpretation.
- Readers interested in deterministic approaches to quantum phenomena.
- Readers searching to connect GR with QM and EM via a common fundamental understanding.
